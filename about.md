I'm Leyton — 24, based in Florida. I build things and write about what I learn
along the way.

My background is hands-on rather than credentialed: I got deep into clinical
dermatology the hard way, through my own experience with low-dose isotretinoin,
and that turned into **[Happyglow](/projects/)** — a premium, physician-guided
telemedicine platform built on the belief that real prescriptions, explained
honestly, beat wellness theater every time.

I'm bootstrapped, no funding, and I'm playing a very long game. The plan isn't
to sell — it's to compound. In chemistry, in business, in habit, the interesting
returns always come from the people who kept showing up.

## What I care about

- **Medicine that's honest about tradeoffs** — no miracle framing, no hype.
- **Building in public** — sharing the real work, not the highlight reel.
- **Craft and longevity** — doing the boring thing well, for a long time.
