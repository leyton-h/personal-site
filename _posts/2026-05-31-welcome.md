I used to keep a personal site on WordPress. It got heavy, I stopped tending it,
and eventually it became one of those tabs you mean to fix and never do. So I
tore it down and rebuilt from scratch — plain Jekyll, hosted on GitHub Pages,
pointed at my own domain. No plugins I don't understand, no dashboard, no
upsells. Just text files I control.

This is the first post, mostly to prove the pipeline works. But here's what I
actually intend to do with this space.

## What this is for

I'm building [Happyglow](/projects/) — a physician-guided telemedicine platform
— and the entire thesis of the brand is that **honest clinical education beats
hype**. It feels right to hold myself to the same standard in public. So expect:

- **Notes on what I'm learning** about medicine, the body, and why so much
  health marketing is quietly dishonest.
- **The real mechanics of building** a bootstrapped company with no funding and
  no plan to sell.
- **Whatever I'm reading** — tracked over on the [bookshelf](/bookshelf/).

## The only rule

Write the way I'd want it explained to me: clearly, without dumbing it down, and
honest about the tradeoffs. If a post doesn't pass that bar, it doesn't go up.

That's the whole plan. The compounding starts now.
